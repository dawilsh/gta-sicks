Fold whole posts away

Renaming alone still leaves a readable article about it — it just removes the word you’d have used to skip past. With this on, a search result, feed post or list item that mentions it collapses to a one-line stub with a show button. Prose on a page you opened deliberately is only renamed, never folded.

Variety

One per page picks a single name and sticks to it, so a page reads consistently. Chaos draws a new one for every mention.

Mark altered text

Wraps every replacement in «guillemets» so you can tell at a glance that the words aren’t the ones on the page. Worth turning on if you’re reading other people’s messages — a live chat, say — where mistaking a rewrite for what someone actually said would be a problem.

Catch the surrounding chatter too

Also replaces Leonida, Vice City, Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. More thorough, occasionally catches an innocent bystander.

Rewrite the tab title

Cleans up your browser tabs and history entries as well as the page.

Rewrite tooltips and image descriptions
